Why it's different

Recommendations that actually know your department

Most platforms show you what's popular everywhere. LearnHub blends three signals so what you see is actually relevant to you.

Matched to your profile

Your department, level, and stated interests are compared against every resource's content to surface what actually fits — even before you've clicked anything.

Trending in your department

Ratings, votes, and downloads from students in your own department surface what's genuinely proving useful right now — not what's popular platform-wide.

Reviewed before it reaches you

Every upload is checked by an admin before it's visible. Alumni and lecturers are verified with real documents before they can vote.
